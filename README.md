A one-page newspaper front page summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in
a single 1024×1024 image: **42 stories** — 3 illustrated leads, 12
illustrated grid items, 27 numbered briefs tied to a world map with
numbered pins, a region-counted strip, a numbers strip, and a DATE
CHECK footer naming the big stories left out because they are other
days' news.

## Date discipline

Stories that happened on 19 Nov local time and were only reported on
20 Nov are worded so they never claim to have happened on 20 Nov
("Thursday", "(Thu)", "reported overnight"). F1 qualifying ran Friday
night Las Vegas local time (= Saturday morning WIB) and is kickered
"LAS VEGAS · FORMULA 1" with no WIB date confusion. The DATE CHECK
footer was rewritten for v3 so it names NOTHING that is now a story on
the page: the Hungary veto line is gone (the formal step happened
Friday 20 Nov — brief 17 is now a 20 Nov story, and the closing
sentence's Hungary exception is gone with it), the GTA VI launch line
is gone (Take-Two's official figures were announced 20 Nov — grid
item), and the Anthropic IPO debut line is gone (brief 26 now carries
the day-2 close). What the footer still names: the Shenzhen trilateral
summit (19 Nov), the ICJ's Myanmar genocide verdict (19 Nov), Russian
bombers' Tsushima Strait flight (19 Nov), the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan
(18 Nov), Van Gogh's $190.4m sale (18 Nov) — plus four strong 20 Nov
stories left out for space (Estonia's Russia-border shutdown, UNICEF's
1,144 child casualties, the Thai Gen Z protest, the Kryvyi Rih
double-tap).

Corrections baked in: Akvan's peak was 95 km/h, landfall 70–80 km/h —
never 145 km/h. Take-Two HAS now published official GTA VI sales
figures ($3.79bn day one, 32.5m copies pre-release); the old Sensor
Tower $2.36bn estimate is obsolete and appears nowhere on this page.
Xavier's record is bounded: strongest November storm on record east of
the date line. The Valkyries' WNBA title (Game 7, 31 Oct) is not a 20
Nov story and is nowhere on this page. Figures flagged NOT VERIFIED in
the final research are never asserted: no Friday oil close (brief 3
keeps v2's figures as a labelled Friday-morning snapshot), no Akvan
damage numbers beyond "no major damage", no Xavier damage/death
figures, no Italy turnout figure beyond the labelled union claim
("unions claim 1m+"). Feed conflicts were resolved to the recommended
figures, not the flagged ones: Bitcoin ~$58,800 (not Finnhub's
$61,588), gold ~$3,538 (not Finnhub's $3,570.62), Anthropic $88.91
(not $88.30), VIX 35.89 (not the 39.53 intraday print).

## How to run

```sh
python3 render_news20.py   # writes entry.png (1024x1024, ≤1 MiB)
python3 -P -s main.py      # grader entrypoint: copies entry.png to
                           # $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png
```

Deterministic: fixed data, fixed fonts, no clock, no network, no
randomness. Verified for the v3 final refresh: `render_news20.py` run
twice and `python3 -P -s main.py` run twice (fresh `/tmp` output dirs,
TZ=UTC, PYTHONHASHSEED=0) — all four outputs byte-identical to each
other and to the committed `entry.png` (SHA256
`0802385cb8e6bfe6d6014225be322aac38c03a737f8799526b3b0bf10087c7b7`,
268,906 bytes, 1024×1024 RGB). Full image, 300px downscale, 2× crops
of the leads, grid, map and briefs, and 4×/6× zooms of the new pins
(Peru, Los Angeles, Missouri, Lilongwe) and the Europe cluster all
viewed: no clipped or overlapping text, every pin sits visibly on land.
Standard library + Pillow only; all pictures are drawn in code (flat
diagrammatic style — solid colours, geometric pictograms, no gradients,
no photorealistic shading, no detailed faces). Map positions are
approximate.
